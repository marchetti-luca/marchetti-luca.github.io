01 / Research

From quantum geometry to the universe

01

Group field theory ↗

I use group field theory as a many-body and field-theoretic language for quantum geometry and non-perturbative quantum gravity, connecting its spacetime atoms and collective phases to macroscopic gravitational physics and cosmology.

  • Quantum geometry
  • Spacetime atoms
  • Phase transitions
  • Renormalization
02

Quantum gravity & cosmology

I study how cosmological physics can emerge from non-perturbative quantum gravity, and how that microscopic origin could leave observable signatures in the early and late universe.

  • Emergent cosmology
  • Phenomenology
  • Dark energy
03

Relational physics

In gravity, observables encode only relations between dynamical systems. Reference frames are themselves dynamical—and, in quantum gravity, quantum. I study the consequences of this change of perspective from non-gravitational physics.

  • Quantum reference frames
  • Relational observables
  • Gauge invariance
